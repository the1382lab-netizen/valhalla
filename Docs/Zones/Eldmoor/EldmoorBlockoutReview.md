# Eldmoor Grasslands - blockout design review (B-06 step 1.5)

Reviewed 2026-09-24 against `EldmoorDesign.md` / `eldmoor_layout.json` (approved 2026-09-24). Reviewed material: the 2048 px top-down capture, the five in-game shots (desert landing, keep gate, Harrow's Rest east gate, Sallow's gap, the cleft), the art-kit renders, `eldmoor_art_kit.md`, `build_eldmoor_blockout.py`, `eldmoor_terrain.py` and `terrain/`. Commits cee5e87e, e1f4f9e3, 406ff276.

Coordinates are zone-local tiles (64 cm), +X east, +Y south, as in the design. "Designer" fixes are changes to `eldmoor_layout.json` (I make them); "builder" fixes are level or script changes; "art" fixes are meshes or materials.

## Verdict

**The blockout matches the approved layout.** Every region, road, track, crossing, camp, thicket, the outpost, the keep with all seven areas, the Desert landing and the cleft are where the design puts them, within a tile. Camp fires are only at the beacons and the cold camps are cold. The keep's chokepoints (gatehouse, inner gate, hall door, barracks door, chapel door, ramp) exist. The builder's eight reported deviations are all acceptable as built, with three data updates on my side so the JSON stops disagreeing with the level.

What does not yet work as designed is small and specific: the hidden cleft is neither hidden nor screened (three separate causes), the zone's north-east corner opens onto a black void, and one landscape join needs checking. Everything else is dressing: the ground is one flat green, the river reads black in the capture, the Downs and Vale are nearly empty, the thickets are topiary, the Tor is a hatched slab, the barrow is a square hut, and the banners are the wrong heraldry.
